# Petai — Creation, Needs & Autonomous Life

> Source: Petai product plan §§4–8, 20.

## Creature creation

Creation is one of the magical moments of the product. The user describes
what they want:

> "A tiny blue dragon that thinks it's a cat, loves pancakes, gets scared
> of thunderstorms and is extremely curious."

AI generates the creature: appearance (species, body, colors, size, eyes,
clothing, accessories, visual style, fantasy characteristics), personality
(playful, shy, chaotic, affectionate, curious, mischievous, calm,
energetic…), preferences (favorite food/games/activities, dislikes, fears,
favorite places), and an optional origin backstory.

The user does **not** get unlimited control over every behavior — see
`02-product-rules.md`.

## Needs — derived, not assigned

Needs (hunger, energy, happiness, socialization, stimulation, affection,
cleanliness, curiosity, boredom…) are **derived from what the creature
is**, not pasted from a template. The generator picks an appropriate need
model per creature kind.

## Caring creates attachment

The user participates: feeding, playing, talking, comforting, cleaning,
giving attention, discovering activities. Classic digital-pet verbs,
deliberately.

## Alive between sessions

When the user closes the app, the creature does **not** freeze. A
lightweight simulation continues. On return, something may have happened:

- "While you were gone, Luna discovered she really likes rain."
- "Your dragon tried to learn a new game."
- "Mochi made a video for you."
- "Your pet missed you."

The user should never know exactly what will happen.

## Autonomous entertainment (not autonomous productivity)

Periodically the creature generates its own entertainment: plays, explores,
draws, sings, tells stories, makes memes, decorates, collects objects,
records a "day in my life", gets into harmless trouble. The loop is:

**leave → creature lives → return → discover**

The app should feel like opening a tiny window into another life.

## Daily loop

Morning: wakes up, checks needs, chooses activity. Day: explores, plays,
socializes, learns preferences, discovers things. Evening: tires, reflects,
maybe creates something. Owner returns: reacts, presents interesting
events, asks for attention if appropriate. Stochastic enough that two
similar pets still develop different lives.
